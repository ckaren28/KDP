Riso Ink Chooser

Drop in your artwork and choose a paper. Every pair of riso inks is tried against your image, ranked by how close it gets, with the colors each pair would lose.

Prototype. Ink colors are screen approximations from the community list on stencil.wiki (via the riso-colors package), and fluorescent inks print brighter than a screen can show. Inks are modeled as see-through filters that multiply. None of this has been checked against a real print yet.
