## The idea

NFTs were having a moment, and the same generative-attribute pattern was a fun, on-brand way to
demo Blocks. You build an avatar, and it travels through the platform: into the store as a
product, onto a spin wheel, and for the winners, a one-to-one booking with the CEO and CTO. All
on one page, so nothing reloads.

## Designing for a crowded room

<p class="why">In a crowded room you get a minute of someone’s attention.</p>

**Arrows, not dropdowns.** Each attribute steps through its options with a pair of arrows:
faster than opening a menu, readable from a distance, and easy with a thumb.

**A curated set.** The design team made far more options than I shipped. Cycling through
everything is exhausting at a conference, so I cut it down.

## Every color pairing checked

Every attribute drew from one conference palette, so two could land on the same hue. I
generated every combination, checked contrast against WCAG 2.1 AA, and blocked the pairings
that failed in code instead of changing the design team’s palette.

## Built in Wix Blocks

I built it in Wix Blocks, still in beta, with one SVG module per attribute, working with the
Blocks product manager and lead engineer. Building on unreleased products surfaced platform
bugs that were fixed before launch.
