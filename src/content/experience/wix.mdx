## The idea

NFTs were having a moment, and I saw the same generative-attribute pattern as a fun, on-brand
way to demo what Blocks could do. You build an avatar, and it travels through the rest of the
platform: into the ecommerce catalog as a product, onto a spin wheel, and for the winners, a
one-to-one booking with the CEO and CTO. All on one page, so nothing reloads.

## Decisions

**Arrows, not dropdowns.** Each attribute steps through its options with a pair of arrows. In a
crowded room you get a minute of someone's attention: stepping is faster than opening a menu,
it reads from a distance, and it works with a thumb on a phone.

**A curated set.** The design team made far more options than I shipped. Cycling through
everything is exhausting at a conference, so I cut it down.

**Every color pairing checked.** Every attribute drew from the same conference palette, so two
could land on the same hue. I generated every combination and checked contrast against WCAG
2.1 AA, then blocked the pairings that failed in code instead of changing the design team's
palette.

## Outcome

**77% of attendees made an avatar.** People shared their avatars on Twitter during the event,
and building on the new releases first surfaced platform bugs that were fixed before launch.
